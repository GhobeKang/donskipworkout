1. Operator and scope

sayudogu (the “operator”) provides DON'T SKIP, a workout attendance and training log service (the “service”). This policy covers account, workout, photo, location, notification and inquiry information processed when you use the service.

Send privacy inquiries to sayudogu@gmail.com.

3. Location information and device permissions

Use of location information

The current version does not provide workout-location arrival reminders or automatically finish workouts when you leave a location. It does not request precise or background location permission. You start, pause, resume and finish workouts manually.

Workout-location information and related state/notifications stored by earlier versions are cleaned up when the app runs after an update. Workout records already stored on the server are retained. Copies in operating system or cloud backups may depend on your device settings.

See Android analytics above for approximate location derived from IP addresses.

Camera, notifications and files

  • Camera: Used when you choose photo check-in. Manual check-in without a photo is also available. Photo check-in is not currently configured to save captured images to your gallery.
  • Notifications: Display workout schedules, challenge updates and host notifications. You can restrict delivery in the app or operating system settings.
  • Shared files: When you choose to share, an image is created in temporary app storage and passed to the operating system's share sheet.

Declining a permission affects its related feature. Notification permission is separate from consent for Android analytics collection.

4. Processing check-in photos

When you capture and submit a photo, an image resized and cropped on your device is sent to the server. The server removes metadata such as EXIF and GPS, then stores both the image before overlays and the image after overlays.

Processed images may include your display name, local capture date/time, a summary of that day's exercises and sets, and decorative elements. Capture time and time zone are sent separately from image EXIF and used to generate the image. Previous check-in photos may be shown to you again according to your reuse settings.

Photo access is restricted to the signed-in account that owns the photo. Current challenge views do not provide participants' check-in photo files or original private notes to other participants. Image hashes are used to detect duplicates. The app does not currently provide facial recognition or biometric identification.

5. Sharing with other users

  • Challenge participants: Other participants in the same challenge can see display names, participation and consent status, goals, check-in dates, progress and assessment results.
  • Challenge hosts: After a challenge period ends, the host can view individual participant progress reports. These may include comparisons of workout performance, duration, exercises, weights and repetitions, scores/assessments, and summaries of changes using past records.
  • Coaching questions and replies: The question author and recipient host can view the conversation. Reported content copies and report descriptions are provided to authorized reviewers.
  • External sharing: When you choose to share or download a report, the selected image is passed to your device or the chosen app/recipient. The recipient's or service's practices govern shared copies.

The app does not currently show account email addresses, passwords, personal photo files or original private notes to other participants. Deleting your app account does not recall copies you have already shared externally.

7. Retention and deletion

Account, workout, check-in photo, challenge and coaching data are retained to provide features while your account exists. User blocking has been discontinued and no new blocking information is collected. Existing blocking records are not used to restrict conversations, notifications or content and are cleaned up when the account is deleted. After a successful account deletion request, linked server records, conversations, notifications, reports of content/users and blocking information are deleted. Original and processed photo files are cleaned up through a separate file deletion task. Operator-managed backups are retained for up to 90 days.

8. Your rights and DON'T SKIP account deletion

You can request deletion of your account and related data by email without installing the app or signing in. sayudogu receives the request, verifies account ownership and emails you the outcome.

Request account deletion by email

  1. Where possible, email sayudogu@gmail.com from the address used to register. Use the subject DON'T SKIP account deletion request.
  2. Include your registered email address and a request to delete your account and all related data. If you cannot access the registered address, provide an address for replies and explain why.
  3. The operator verifies account ownership. If verification is difficult, only the minimum additional information needed will be requested. Do not send passwords or copies of identity documents.
  4. After verification, the operator deletes the account and linked server data and informs you of the result. If further verification or a delay is needed, the reason and expected processing schedule will be explained.

Deletion scope: Account/authentication information and all sign-in sessions; workout schedules, records, notes and timers; check-in photos; related coaching conversations, notifications, content/user reports, blocking records and challenge participation; and copies of that user's assessments and progress reports. Other participants' accounts and workout records are not deleted.

Retention exceptions: Operator-managed backups are destroyed after a maximum of 90 days. Photo files are handled by a separate deletion task. Externally shared copies and provider-managed information may not be recalled immediately by this request alone. Any identified separate legal retention obligation will be explained with its categories, grounds and duration.

You can also use the same email address to request access, correction, deletion of specific data, suspension of processing or withdrawal of consent.

You can change workout schedules and notifications in app settings and withdraw camera/notification permission in device settings.

In the app, open Settings → Delete account at the bottom, then enter your current password and the confirmation text DELETE. Email deletion requests do not require reinstalling the app, signing in or accepting new terms.

After successful account deletion, the app displays the result and deletes all sign-in sessions. It also requests cleanup of account-specific temporary records and notifications on the current device. Local copies on other offline devices, backups and provider data may be affected by separate processing procedures. Uninstalling or signing out alone does not delete your account. You may need to contact recipients separately about copies already shared externally.

9. Security measures

The current service uses password hashing, restrictions on script access to sign-in cookies, account-specific access checks, photo ownership checks, request rate limits, removal of photo metadata, TLS for database connections, and practices that avoid directly logging passwords or request bodies.

Personal information and deletion requests are handled only by the operator and authorized personnel who need access. The operator checks deletion or transmission failures. We do not request passwords, unnecessary identity documents or health information by email.

10. Children's information

The service is intended for all ages, and this policy applies to all users, including children. The current registration screen does not verify dates of birth or obtain consent from legal guardians.

Where consent is required to process the personal information of a child under 14, a legal guardian's consent and verification are necessary. Guardians may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing by emailing sayudogu@gmail.com. If children's information processed without appropriate consent is identified, the operator will take necessary steps, including stopping processing and deleting the information.

Frequently asked questions

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check workout days, times and notification preferences in app settings, and make sure device settings allow notifications for DON'T SKIP. Focus, Do Not Disturb and other device settings may limit notification display.

How do I record exercises and sets?

In Workout, choose a built-in exercise or create your own. Specify the name, main target area and optional detailed area, then enter weights, repetitions and sets. Set records save automatically as you enter them. An account and an internet connection are required.

Why does a report say there are insufficient comparison records?

Reports need workout dates, exercises and set records. Performance changes use comparable conditions, such as the same weight or repetitions. The activity score uses recorded exercises and sets; it does not measure actual muscle stimulation.

How do I start a challenge with friends?

In My Challenges, create a room or join using an invite code. Community participation requires acceptance of the in-app terms.

How do I delete my account and data?

Use Delete account at the bottom of Settings, or request deletion by email without installing the app or signing in. Read the deletion scope and request steps.
